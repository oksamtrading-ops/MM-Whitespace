# Object 3 — the claim grid

**What it is.** A tilted lattice of mineral-claim cells, as drawn on a
provincial claims map, seen at an angle so it recedes. One cell is the brand
green; the rest are outlines.

**Material.** Ink on a black ground. Hairline cool grey 10 `#63666A` for the
lattice, one cell filled `#86BC25`, no shading. It is a drawing, not a
surface; depth comes from the perspective alone.

**Palette.** Cool grey 10, brand green, black. On a light ground the lattice
is cool grey 4 `#BBBCBC` and the filled cell stays brand green.

**Lighting.** None. A drawing has no light.

**Camera.** 30° tilt on the horizontal axis, the vanishing point above the
frame, the filled cell in the lower third where the lattice is largest.

**Allowed angles.** Static, or one settle from 30° to 0° over 600ms when it
stands in for the map on a page that has no map data (the same collapse the
map itself performs). Flat under reduced motion.

**Where.** The dashboard's "no period yet" and "not published" refusals; the
companies page when the population is empty. Never beside a real map.

**Files.** SVG only — it is lines, and an SVG is the smallest and sharpest
form. Under 12 KB.
